# Privacy policy

_Effective: <date of first release>. App: Maintenance Tracker (working name). Developer: <name / contact email>._

**Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect, receive or sell any of it.**

## What the app stores

Vehicles, maintenance schedules, service records, costs, parts, meter readings, and any photos
or documents you add. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you do it yourself:

- **Export.** When you export a backup or spreadsheet, the app writes a file to a location you
  choose (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app). What happens to that file
  afterwards is up to you and that location's provider.
- **Opening a document.** When you open a stored photo or PDF, the app hands that one file to
  the viewer app you pick.
- **Purchases.** Buying Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. The app learns only whether Pro
  is unlocked; Google's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

## What we don't do

No account or sign-in. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No advertising. No servers of
ours receive any data. The app needs no internet connection except for Google Play purchases.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to remind you when maintenance is due.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)** — so reminders arrive at the time you set.
- **Run at startup** — to re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts.
- The camera is used only through your phone's own camera app, when you choose to take a photo.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Delete all data removes everything from the device. Uninstalling the app
also deletes it. Android's own device backup is turned off for this app, so nothing is copied
to Google's backup servers; use the app's export if you want a backup.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
