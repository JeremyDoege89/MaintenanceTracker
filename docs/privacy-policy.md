# Privacy policy

_Effective: October 6, 2026. App: Maintenance Tracker for Android, by the developer named on its Google Play listing._

**Short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect, receive or sell any of it.
The one exception is a VIN you choose to decode, which goes to the US government's free VIN decoder.**

## What the app stores

Vehicles, maintenance schedules, service records, costs, parts, meter readings, and any photos
or documents you add. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you do it yourself:

- **Export.** When you export a backup or spreadsheet, the app writes a file to a location you
  choose (for example your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app). What happens to that file
  afterwards is up to you and that location's provider.
- **Opening a document.** When you open a stored photo or PDF, the app hands that one file to
  the viewer app you pick.
- **VIN decode.** When you tap **Decode** next to a VIN, the app sends that VIN (and the model
  year, if you typed one) to the free vPIC decoder run by the US National Highway Traffic Safety
  Administration (NHTSA) at vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov, and gets back the make, model and year. Nothing
  else is sent: no name, no other vehicle details, no identifier for you or your phone. The
  answer is kept on your phone so the same VIN isn't sent twice. NHTSA's own privacy policy
  covers its servers. If you never tap Decode, no VIN ever leaves the device. Boat HINs are
  never sent anywhere.
- **Scanning a VIN or HIN.** If you use the scan button, the camera picture is read on your phone
  by Google's ML Kit, built into the app. Pictures and the text read from them are never saved or
  sent; only the number you confirm goes into the VIN/HIN field. ML Kit may send Google basic
  diagnostic and usage information about the scanner itself (not pictures or scanned text);
  Google's ML Kit terms cover that.
- **Purchases.** Buying Pro is handled entirely by Google Play. The app learns only whether Pro
  is unlocked; Google's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

## What we don't do

No account or sign-in. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (ML Kit's own diagnostics are described above). No advertising. No servers of
ours receive any data. The app needs no internet connection except for Google Play purchases, the optional VIN decode,
and ML Kit's diagnostics described above.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — to remind you when maintenance is due.
- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)** — so reminders arrive at the time you set.
- **Run at startup** — to re-schedule reminders after the phone restarts.
- **Internet** — only for the optional VIN decode, Google Play purchases and ML Kit's diagnostics.
- **Camera** — only for the optional VIN/HIN scan, asked the first time you tap it. Photos you
  attach are still taken through your phone's own camera app.

## Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Delete all data removes everything from the device, including saved VIN decodes. Uninstalling the app
also deletes it. Android's own device backup is turned off for this app, so nothing is copied
to Google's backup servers; use the app's export if you want a backup.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
